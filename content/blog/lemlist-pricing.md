Lemlist currently advertises two main plans: Email at around $69/month billed monthly (roughly $55/month on annual billing), priced per workspace, and Multichannel at around $109 per user per month (roughly $87 on annual billing), plus a custom-priced Enterprise tier. The catch is that the Multichannel plan — the one with LinkedIn automation, which is lemlist's headline feature — is priced per user, so a three-person team pays roughly three times the sticker price before any credits or add-ons. Once you factor in email-finder and verification credits, extra sending accounts and phone-number lookups, many teams end up paying noticeably more than the advertised number.

Prices in this article were checked against lemlist's own pricing page in July 2026, but lemlist has restructured its plans more than once in recent years — always confirm on their site before buying.

## Lemlist's advertised plans

Here is what lemlist publishes, as of mid-2026:

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | Priced per | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email | ~$69/mo | ~$55/mo | Workspace | Up to ~50,000 emails/month, unlimited users and senders, lead database access, email/phone finder (credit-based), warm-up and deliverability tools |
| Multichannel | ~$109/user/mo | ~$87/user/mo | User (seat) | Everything in Email plus LinkedIn automation, call dialler, SMS/WhatsApp options, ~5 senders per user, unlimited sends |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | User (5+ seats minimum) | Multichannel plus CRM integration, SSO/SAML, custom roles, dedicated account manager |

A few things worth noting before you compare this with other tools:

- **The Email plan is now per workspace, not per seat.** That is a genuinely good deal for teams that only send email: unlimited users on one subscription, capped at around 50,000 emails a month. Lemlist changed this relatively recently, and plenty of third-party pricing articles still describe an older per-seat "Email Pro" structure — trust the current pricing page, not cached blog posts.
- **Multichannel — the plan with LinkedIn — is still per user.** This is where team costs escalate, and it is the plan most agencies and multichannel teams actually need.
- **The trial is 14 days** with full Multichannel access, no card required.

## What's per user and what's shared: the team-cost multiplier

If your team only needs email, lemlist's workspace pricing is one of the more team-friendly structures in the market. The problem starts the moment you want LinkedIn steps in your sequences, because that requires Multichannel, and Multichannel multiplies by headcount.

At the advertised monthly rate of roughly $109 per user:

| Team size | Multichannel, monthly billing | Multichannel, annual billing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 user | ~$109/mo | ~$87/mo |
| 3 users | ~$327/mo | ~$261/mo |
| 5 users | ~$545/mo | ~$435/mo |

So a three-person team running multichannel outreach is looking at roughly $327/month on monthly billing — in the region of £250/month at typical exchange rates — before a single credit or add-on. Annual billing softens that to around $261/month, but you are committing a year upfront to get it.

The exchange-rate point matters for UK buyers: lemlist bills in dollars, so your actual cost in pounds moves with the currency and your card provider's conversion fee. If you are budgeting in GBP, add a small buffer.

## The add-ons and credits that stack on top

Lemlist bundles a lot into its plans, but several of the things you will actually use day-to-day are metered separately:

- **Email finding and verification credits.** Lemlist's lead database and email finder run on a credit system. Credits are only charged when a lookup succeeds, at roughly $10 per 1,000 credits, with a verified email typically costing around 5 credits (about $0.05) and a phone number around 20 credits (about $0.20). Plans include a monthly credit allowance, but teams doing real prospecting volume routinely buy top-ups.
- **Extra sending accounts.** Multichannel includes a set number of senders per user (around five at the time of writing). Third-party pricing breakdowns report additional sending addresses at roughly $9 per mailbox per month — worth confirming on your own account, but if you follow the standard playbook of spreading volume across many mailboxes (see our guide to [cold email sending limits](/blog/cold-email-sending-limits-google-vs-microsoft/)), this line item grows quickly.
- **Intent signals and calling.** Buying-signal lookups are credit-priced (reported at 20–400+ credits per signal depending on type), and the in-app dialler runs on usage-based calling credits.
- **LinkedIn features gated to the higher tier.** There is no cheap way to add LinkedIn to the Email plan — LinkedIn steps, voice notes and profile-visit automation all live on Multichannel, at the full per-user price.

Several independent pricing teardowns estimate that the realistic all-in cost of running lemlist — credits, extra senders, verification — lands meaningfully above sticker price, with some putting the gap at 30–50% for active teams. Treat that as a directional estimate rather than a promise, but budget for the pattern: the subscription is the floor, not the total.

## The Chrome extension and LinkedIn reliability

Lemlist's LinkedIn automation depends partly on a Chrome extension, and this is the most consistent complaint thread in user reviews. Some reviewers on G2 and Capterra report the extension disconnecting, LinkedIn steps stalling until someone notices, and sequences silently pausing — which matters when you are paying per seat specifically for the LinkedIn functionality. Others run it for months without trouble, so this is not universal, but it is common enough to test hard during your trial: run a real LinkedIn sequence for the full 14 days and watch whether steps execute on schedule without babysitting.

It is also worth remembering that any LinkedIn automation — lemlist's or anyone's — has to respect LinkedIn's tolerance for automated activity, including the widely-observed norm of roughly 100 connection invites per week. A tool that lets you exceed sensible limits is a liability, not a feature. We cover the trade-offs in more depth in [cold email vs LinkedIn outreach](/blog/cold-email-vs-linkedin-outreach/).

## Who lemlist genuinely suits

A fair teardown should say what lemlist is good at, because it is a serious product:

- **Image and video personalisation.** Lemlist essentially pioneered personalised images and landing pages in cold email, and it remains one of the best implementations. If dynamic screenshots and custom thumbnails are core to your outreach style, this alone can justify the price.
- **True multichannel sequencing.** Email, LinkedIn, calls and SMS in one sequence builder, with conditional steps, is more than most rivals offer natively.
- **Community and templates.** Lemlist's template library and its founder-led community are genuinely useful, especially for people writing their first sequences (though you should still read our guide to [cold email templates that get replies](/blog/cold-email-templates-that-get-replies/) before copying anything wholesale).
- **The per-workspace Email plan** is strong value for email-only teams of three or more people who do not need LinkedIn steps.

Where it fits less well: solo founders who find $109/month steep for multichannel, UK teams uncomfortable with dollar billing and credit top-ups, and anyone who wants predictable per-month costs regardless of headcount. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much is lemlist per month?

As of mid-2026, lemlist's Email plan costs around $69/month billed monthly (about $55/month on annual billing) and covers a whole workspace with roughly a 50,000 email/month cap. The Multichannel plan, which adds LinkedIn, calls and SMS, costs around $109 per user per month (about $87 annually). Enterprise pricing is custom with a five-seat minimum.

### Does lemlist charge per user?

It depends on the plan. The Email plan is currently priced per workspace with unlimited users, which is good value for email-only teams. The Multichannel plan — required for LinkedIn automation — is priced per user, so a three-person team pays roughly three times the advertised seat price.

### What extra costs should I budget for on top of lemlist's subscription?

The main ones are lead-database and email-finder credits (roughly $10 per 1,000, with a verified email around $0.05 and a phone number around $0.20), extra sending mailboxes beyond your plan's allowance (reported at about $9/mailbox/month), and usage-based calling credits. Active teams commonly spend meaningfully above sticker price once these stack up, so treat the subscription as a floor.

### Does lemlist have a free plan or trial?

Lemlist offers a 14-day free trial with full access to the Multichannel plan and no credit card required. There is no permanent free plan, though the lead database and some deliverability tooling can be explored during the trial.

### Is lemlist worth it?

For teams that lean heavily on image/video personalisation or want email, LinkedIn, calls and SMS in one sequence builder, lemlist is a genuinely strong product and the workspace-priced Email plan is good value. It is harder to justify for solo founders or small UK teams on a budget, where per-user Multichannel pricing plus dollar billing plus credit top-ups can be several times the cost of a flat-price alternative.

### Is lemlist cheaper than Velox House?

Generally not for multichannel teams. Lemlist Multichannel for three users runs roughly $327/month on monthly billing (around £250), while Velox House Growth is a flat £49.99/month including 3 seats and a LinkedIn seat, with verification included rather than credit-metered. Lemlist's per-workspace Email plan is more competitive for email-only teams, but it excludes LinkedIn entirely.
