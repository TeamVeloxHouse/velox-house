## Why people look for Instantly alternatives

First, credit where it's due. Instantly is a genuinely strong product: it made unlimited email accounts the industry norm, its interface is quick to learn, and it publishes useful benchmark data on cold email performance. Plenty of teams are happy on it.

But recurring themes show up in public reviews on G2 and Trustpilot from users who moved on:

- **Total cost scales faster than expected.** The advertised entry price (~$37/month) covers sending only. Users report that once you add the lead database, verification and a realistic contact volume, real monthly spend often lands at $180+ — a very different number from the one on the pricing page.
- **Support quality on paid tiers.** A common complaint is reaching an AI chatbot rather than a person, even on higher plans, with slow escalation when a deliverability issue is burning a domain.
- **Shared warm-up pool concerns.** Instantly's warm-up network is large, which is a strength — but some users worry that a shared pool used by tens of thousands of senders (including careless ones) carries reputation risk, and that mailbox providers increasingly detect warm-up traffic patterns.
- **Lead database accuracy.** Reviews report mixed hit rates on emails from the built-in database, which matters when every bounce chips away at your sender reputation (see our [deliverability guide](/blog/cold-email-deliverability-guide/) for why bounce rate is the metric to protect).

None of these are dealbreakers for every team. But if one of them is your dealbreaker, here are the tools that fix it.

## 2. Smartlead — best for agencies

Smartlead (from ~$39/month) is the closest like-for-like Instantly replacement: unlimited email accounts, unlimited warm-up, and strong infrastructure features like inbox rotation across huge mailbox counts and a unified master inbox across clients. Its API is well regarded, and agencies running dozens of client campaigns tend to land here.

**Weaknesses:** no built-in lead database (you'll pay separately for data), and the interface is more functional than friendly — expect a learning curve. Support reviews are mixed at peak times.

**Verdict:** the agency workhorse. If you're managing many domains and clients, it's probably your shortlist leader.

## 3. Saleshandy — best on a tight budget

Saleshandy (from ~$25–29/month) undercuts almost everyone while keeping the essentials: unlimited email accounts, unified inbox, sequence automation and sender rotation. A lead-finder database is available as a paid add-on rather than bundled.

**Weaknesses:** the AI-writing and personalisation features are thinner than Lemlist's or Velox's, and reporting is basic. Data quality on the add-on database gets mixed reviews.

**Verdict:** the value pick if you already have lead lists and just need reliable sending for the lowest possible monthly cost.

**Weaknesses:** per-user pricing gets expensive as a team grows, and reviewers note the platform has become busier and more complex over the years. If you left Instantly over price, Lemlist rarely fixes that. We compare it in more depth in our [Lemlist alternatives](/blog/lemlist-alternatives/) guide.

## 5. Woodpecker — best for deliverability-first simplicity

Woodpecker (from ~$24–35/month, priced per prospects contacted) has spent a decade focused on one thing: emails that land. Features like automatic bounce detection, human-like sending patterns, and condition-based follow-ups are all tuned for inbox placement over volume.

**Weaknesses:** no built-in lead database, per-prospect pricing that needs watching at scale, and lighter AI features than newer rivals.

**Verdict:** best for solo founders and small teams who want a safe, simple sender and are happy to source leads elsewhere.

## 6. Reply.io — best for multichannel plus calling

Reply.io (from ~$49/user/month) covers email, LinkedIn automation, WhatsApp, SMS and a built-in dialler — the widest channel spread on this list. Its AI assistant handles drafting and reply categorisation, and a B2B database is available as an add-on.

**Weaknesses:** per-user pricing adds up, and doing many things means doing none quite as deeply as the specialists — its email deliverability tooling trails Woodpecker's, its personalisation trails Lemlist's.

**Verdict:** best for sales teams that genuinely work phones alongside email and LinkedIn (worth reading our take on [email vs LinkedIn outreach](/blog/cold-email-vs-linkedin-outreach/) first).

## 7. Apollo — best free start and biggest database

Data accuracy outside the US gets mixed reviews; UK-focused teams should test a sample first (we cover this in [Apollo alternatives for UK teams](/blog/apollo-alternatives-uk/)).

**Verdict:** best when finding contacts is your bottleneck and sending volume is modest.

## Migrating from Instantly: three practical notes

1. **Export your leads first.** Instantly lets you export campaign contacts to CSV — do this before downgrading, and bring reply/interest status with you so you don't re-email people who said no.
2. **Your warm domains carry over; warm-up history doesn't.** Domain and mailbox reputation lives with the mailbox provider, not the tool — a domain that lands in inboxes today still will tomorrow. But ramp gently on the new platform anyway and keep daily volume conservative for the first fortnight (our guide to [how many cold emails per day](/blog/how-many-cold-emails-per-day/) has sensible ramp numbers).
3. **Re-verify old lists.** Emails decay fast as people change jobs. Any list older than a couple of months should be re-verified before it goes near your new tool — bounces on day one is the worst possible start with a new sending setup.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best Instantly.ai alternative?

It depends on why you're switching. For UK SMBs who want AI to run the whole campaign with verified contacts included, Velox House (from £19.99/month) is the strongest fit. Agencies with many inboxes usually pick Smartlead, budget-first teams pick Saleshandy, and database-first teams pick Apollo.

### Is Instantly.ai worth it?

For many teams, yes — it's a polished sender with unlimited email accounts and a big warm-up network. The caution is total cost: users report that with the lead database and verification added, real spend often reaches $180+/month versus the ~$37 advertised, so budget on the full stack, not the entry price.

### What's the cheapest Instantly alternative?

Saleshandy (from ~$25/month) and Woodpecker (from ~$24/month) have the lowest entry prices for pure sending. If you also need lead data, Velox House Starter at £19.99/month includes 300 SMTP-verified contacts and AI writing, which usually works out cheaper than a sending tool plus a separate database subscription.

### Do Instantly alternatives include lead databases?

Some do. Velox House sources and SMTP-verifies contacts on every plan, Lemlist bundles a B2B database, and Apollo is a database first and a sender second. Smartlead, Saleshandy (base plans) and Woodpecker are sending tools — you bring your own leads or pay for a data add-on.

### Will my domain reputation transfer if I switch tools?

Yes. Sender reputation attaches to your domain and mailbox at the provider level (Google, Microsoft), not to the software you send from — so warm domains stay warm when you switch. Ramp volume gradually on the new platform anyway, and make sure SPF, DKIM and DMARC still pass; you can check them free at [/tools](/tools).

### Is a shared warm-up pool risky?

It's debated. Large pools warm inboxes quickly, but you share the pool's reputation with every other sender in it, and mailbox providers have become better at spotting warm-up traffic patterns. The lower-risk route is conservative daily volumes from your own established mailbox — which is why some tools, including Velox House, send only from your own inbox at natural pacing.
