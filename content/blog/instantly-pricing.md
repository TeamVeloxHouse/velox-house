Instantly.ai's sending plans start at $47/month (Growth) and $97/month (Hypergrowth), but that headline price only buys the sending engine. Lead database access, email enrichment and verification run on a separate credits subscription that starts at another $47/month, so a working setup for most users lands between roughly $94 and $294/month — more if you need Instantly's done-for-you services or dedicated infrastructure. Prices here were checked against instantly.ai in July 2026, but they change often, so always confirm on the vendor's site before buying.

## Instantly's advertised plans at a glance

Instantly splits its pricing into three families: **outreach plans** (the sending tool), **credits plans** (the lead database and AI tools), and **bundles** that combine the two. That structure is the single most important thing to understand before comparing it with anything else, because the number most people quote — $37–47/month — is only one of the two subscriptions you'll probably need.

| Plan | Monthly price | What it is | Key limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Growth | $47/mo (~$37.60/mo annual) | Sending only | 1,000 uploaded contacts, 5,000 emails/mo |
| Hypergrowth | $97/mo (~$77.60/mo annual) | Sending only | 25,000 contacts, ~100,000+ emails/mo |
| Lightspeed | $358/mo | Sending only | 100,000 contacts, 500,000 emails/mo |
| Growth Credits | $47/mo | Leads + tools | 1,500 credits/mo, lead database access |
| Supersonic Credits | $197/mo | Leads + tools | 5,000 credits/mo |
| Starter bundle | $94/mo ($85 annual) | Sending + credits | 1,000 contacts, 5,000 emails, 1,500 credits |
| Scale bundle | $194/mo ($175 annual) | Sending + credits | 25,000 contacts, 100,000 emails, 5,000 credits |
| Agency bundle | $555/mo ($500 annual) | Sending + credits | 100,000 contacts, 500,000 emails, 10,000 credits |

All figures from instantly.ai's pricing page at the time of writing; annual billing discounts of roughly 20% apply across most tiers. Check the vendor site — prices change.

## What each sending tier actually includes

The genuinely generous part of Instantly's model is unlimited email accounts and unlimited warm-up on every sending plan, including the cheapest. If your strategy is "many mailboxes, modest volume per mailbox" — which is the right strategy, as we cover in our [sending limits guide](/blog/cold-email-sending-limits-google-vs-microsoft/) — that's a real cost advantage over tools that charge per mailbox.

- **Growth ($47/mo)** covers 1,000 uploaded contacts and 5,000 emails a month with chat support. The contact cap is the practical ceiling: 1,000 active contacts is small once you're running multi-step sequences, and it's the limit most users hit first.
- **Hypergrowth ($97/mo)** lifts you to 25,000 contacts and a six-figure monthly email allowance (Instantly's own pages have listed both 100,000 and 125,000 — check current terms), plus premium live support. This is the tier most consistent senders end up on.
- **Lightspeed ($358/mo)** is for serious volume: 500,000 emails a month and Instantly's dedicated sending infrastructure (its "SISR" system).

None of these include leads. Uploading your own CSV is fine, but if you expected the tool to *find* prospects for you, that's the second subscription.

## The extras that stack on top

This is where the real monthly cost diverges from the pricing-page headline.

**Lead database credits.** Instantly's B2B database (450M+ contacts advertised) is gated behind the credits plans: $47/month buys 1,500 credits, $197/month buys 5,000, with higher tiers beyond that. Credits are consumed when you reveal and export leads, enrich them, or use the AI agents (web researcher, email writer). If you send steadily, 1,500 credits a month disappears quickly.

**Email verification.** Verifying addresses before you send — essential for deliverability, as bounces above ~2% damage sender reputation fast — also draws on credits rather than being free with a sending plan. Budget for it, or verify through a third-party tool, but either way it's a real cost that isn't in the $47 figure. Our [deliverability guide](/blog/cold-email-deliverability-guide/) covers why skipping verification is a false economy.

**Done-for-you services.** Instantly sells a VIP tier (custom pricing) covering campaign setup, inbox management, a dedicated account manager and a shared Slack channel. Useful for teams with budget and no time, but it's quote-based, so factor in a sales call.

**Warm-up: included, with a caveat.** Unlimited warm-up on every plan is genuinely good value. That said, users on G2 and Reddit have raised questions about large shared warm-up pools — the concern being that mailbox providers can fingerprint warm-up network patterns. Whether warm-up still moves the needle at all in 2026 is its own debate, which we dig into in [does email warm-up still work?](/blog/does-email-warmup-still-work/). Treat warm-up as a supplement to proper authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), not a substitute.

## The real monthly maths: three worked scenarios

Hedged numbers, monthly billing, and assuming you want Instantly to supply leads as well as send. Convert to sterling at whatever the exchange rate is doing this month — roughly speaking, $94 is in the £70–80 range.

| Scenario | What you need | Approx. monthly total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo founder | Growth sending ($47) + Growth Credits ($47) — or the Starter bundle at $94 | ~$94/mo (~£70–80) |
| Small team (2–3 senders, steady volume) | Hypergrowth ($97) + Growth or Supersonic Credits ($47–197) | ~$144–294/mo (~£110–230) |
| Agency (multiple clients) | Lightspeed ($358) + Hyper Credits (from $197), or the Agency bundle | ~$555+/mo (~£430+) |

Two things worth noticing. First, the Starter bundle at $94/month is simply the two $47 subscriptions packaged together — there's no discount for bundling monthly, only for going annual. Second, the jump from solo to small team is steep: the moment you outgrow 1,000 contacts or 1,500 credits, your bill can double or triple in one upgrade. ## Who Instantly is genuinely good for

Being fair: Instantly is a strong product with a deserved user base. It's a good fit if you:

- **Run high email volume across many mailboxes.** Unlimited accounts and warm-up on every plan makes multi-inbox scaling cheap, and the Lightspeed/Agency tiers handle genuinely large sending operations.
- **Want benchmark data.** Instantly publishes useful aggregate deliverability and reply-rate data from its user base, which is handy for sanity-checking your own numbers.
- **Are email-only by design.** If LinkedIn isn't part of your motion and you just want a fast, focused sending engine, Instantly does that job well — see our [cold email vs LinkedIn comparison](/blog/cold-email-vs-linkedin-outreach/) if you're weighing that up.
- **Have a US-priced budget.** Everything is billed in dollars; fine for most, but UK buyers carry exchange-rate wobble and card fees on top.

Where it fits less well: users who wanted one price that includes leads, verification and sending (the modular model works against you), small teams sensitive to the $47→$294 scaling cliff, and anyone who wants multichannel sequences in the same tool.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Instantly really cost per month?

For most users, $94–294/month. The $47 Growth plan only covers sending; lead database access and verification credits are a separate subscription from $47/month, so a solo founder typically pays around $94/month (the Starter bundle) and a small team on Hypergrowth plus credits pays $144–294/month. Always check instantly.ai for current prices — they change.

### Does Instantly have a free trial?

Yes — Instantly has historically offered a free trial (commonly 14 days) on its sending plans, no card details required to start in most cases. Trial terms change, so confirm the current offer on instantly.ai's pricing page before signing up.

### Is Instantly's warm-up really unlimited and free?

Warm-up is included on every sending plan with no per-mailbox charge, which is genuinely good value. Users on G2 and Reddit have raised concerns about large shared warm-up pools being detectable by mailbox providers, so treat warm-up as one deliverability input alongside SPF, DKIM, DMARC and list verification, not a fix on its own.

### Are Instantly's lead database credits included in the sending plans?

No. The lead database, enrichment and AI agents run on separate credits plans starting at $47/month for 1,500 credits. Credits are consumed when you reveal, export, enrich or verify leads, so budget them as a recurring cost, not a one-off.

### Is Instantly cheaper on annual billing?

Yes — annual billing cuts roughly 20% off most tiers, taking Growth from $47 to about $37.60/month and the Starter bundle from $94 to $85/month. The trade-off is a year's commitment in dollars; monthly billing costs more but lets you leave when you like.

### Is Instantly worth it for a UK small business?

If you run high-volume, email-only outreach across many mailboxes, yes — unlimited accounts and warm-up scale cheaply.
