## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best cold email software for UK businesses in 2026?

For most UK founders and small B2B teams, Velox House is the strongest fit: GBP pricing from £19.99/month, SMTP-verified contacts, unlimited sending from your own inbox, and LinkedIn included from the £49.99 plan. Apollo suits teams needing a huge US database, and Instantly or Smartlead suit high-volume inbox rotation. The right answer depends on volume, market and budget.

### Why do cold email tools end up costing more than advertised?

The advertised price usually covers sending only. Lead data, email verification, warm-up, extra users and LinkedIn are commonly sold as add-ons or credits, and users report on G2 and Trustpilot that real spend often lands two to four times the headline price. Always price the full stack — sending, data, verification and any LinkedIn seats — before comparing tools.

### Is cold email legal in the UK?

Yes, business-to-business cold email is lawful in the UK when done properly. PECR treats corporate subscribers (limited companies, LLPs) differently from individuals, and UK GDPR's legitimate-interests basis can cover relevant, targeted B2B outreach with a clear opt-out. Sole traders and partnerships are treated like consumers, so they need consent. Our guide to UK cold email law covers the details.

### Do I need separate tools for cold email and LinkedIn outreach?

Not necessarily. Most cold email tools are email-only, so adding LinkedIn traditionally meant buying a second product. A few platforms combine both — Velox House includes a LinkedIn seat from its £49.99 Growth plan and sequences the two channels together, pausing automatically when a prospect replies. If you buy separate tools, you'll coordinate stop-conditions and timing yourself.

### How important is email verification when choosing a tool?

Very. Bounces are the fastest way to damage sender reputation, and Google and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules mean poor list hygiene now gets domains filtered quickly. Prefer tools that verify contacts before they enter your list rather than selling verification as an optional extra — and keep bounce rates low by never importing unverified purchased lists.

### Should I pick a tool with a built-in lead database?

Only if the database is accurate for your market. Built-in databases skew heavily towards US contacts, and users report accuracy dropping for UK and European SMBs — wrong emails mean bounces, which hurt deliverability. For UK-focused outreach, verified sourcing matters more than raw database size; a smaller list of confirmed-valid contacts outperforms a big list of guesses.
