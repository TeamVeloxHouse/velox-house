## Why people look for a lemlist alternative

First, credit where it's due. lemlist pioneered image and video personalisation in cold email — the "your prospect's name on a coffee cup" screenshot that half the industry copied — and it was earlier than most to proper multichannel sequences combining email and LinkedIn. It remains a polished, capable platform, and if it's working for you at your team size, there's no urgent reason to leave.

That said, recurring themes show up in public reviews on G2, Capterra and Reddit when users explain why they moved:

- **Per-user pricing gets expensive for teams.** lemlist's multichannel plans list at roughly $79–109 per user per month (check their site for current figures — pricing changes). Users report the real cost often lands 30–50% higher once you add enrichment credits, extra sending accounts and add-ons. For a solo founder that's manageable; for a team of three to five it's a four-figure annual line item that flat-priced rivals undercut heavily.
- **Chrome extension instability.** The LinkedIn features run through a browser extension, and reviewers report it disconnecting, missing steps or needing regular re-authentication — annoying when your sequence silently stalls.
- **Credit-system opacity.** Enrichment, email finding and verification draw from a credit pool, and users report finding it hard to predict what a campaign will actually consume — or discovering mid-month that they've run out.
- **LinkedIn features are shallower than dedicated tools.** Reviewers who lean heavily on LinkedIn outreach often say the LinkedIn side feels like an add-on to an email tool rather than a first-class channel.

If any of those stung, here are the seven alternatives worth your shortlist.

The same logic applies as you grow: Velox Scale (£79.99) covers five seats for less than one lemlist seat costs.

Ask three questions:

1. **Who does the work — you or the tool?** If you enjoy building sequences, testing copy and managing lead lists, Instantly, Smartlead or Saleshandy give you excellent manual controls cheaply. If you'd rather state the outcome and approve what the AI produces, Velox House is built around that. 3. **What does your team look like in a year?** Price the tool at your team size in twelve months, not today. Per-user pricing punishes growth; flat pricing doesn't.

## Migration notes: leaving lemlist cleanly

Switching costs an afternoon, not a week, if you do it in order:

- **Export everything first** — leads, campaign stats and especially your suppression/unsubscribe list. Import the suppression list into the new tool *before* your first send; emailing someone who opted out is both rude and a UK GDPR/PECR problem.
- **Don't migrate mid-sequence.** Let active sequences finish or stop them deliberately; two tools working the same list risks double-touching prospects.
- **Reconnect mailboxes, don't recreate them.** Sender reputation lives with your domain and mailbox, not the tool, so the same inbox carries its history to the new platform. Run your domain through a free [deliverability checker](/tools) to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC are healthy before resuming volume.
- **Rebuild, don't paste.** Sequence logic rarely maps one-to-one between tools. Treat the move as a chance to cut steps that never earned replies — [these templates](/blog/cold-email-templates-that-get-replies/) are a good starting point.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do people switch away from lemlist?

The most common reasons cited in public reviews on G2, Capterra and Reddit are per-user pricing that gets expensive for teams (~$79–109/user/mo, often 30–50% more in practice with credits and add-ons), instability in the Chrome extension powering LinkedIn features, hard-to-predict credit consumption, and LinkedIn functionality shallower than dedicated tools. The complaints are mostly about cost structure and reliability, not core capability.

### What is the cheapest lemlist alternative?

On headline price, Woodpecker and Saleshandy start around $24–29/mo, and Velox House Starter is £19.99/mo with unlimited email sending. Apollo technically has a free tier, though it's limited. For teams, the cheapest option is usually whichever tool charges flat rather than per user: three people on Velox House Growth costs £49.99/mo total versus roughly $237–327/mo for three lemlist seats.

### Which lemlist alternative is best for LinkedIn outreach?

Velox House includes a cloud-based LinkedIn seat from its Growth plan (£49.99/mo) and runs email and LinkedIn in one sequence, stopping when a prospect replies. Reply.io also has built-in LinkedIn automation on per-user pricing. Instantly, Smartlead and Saleshandy are email-first, so if LinkedIn is central to your outreach they're the wrong shortlist.

### Can I move my lemlist campaigns to another tool?

Yes — export your leads, results and suppression list from lemlist, then import them into the new platform, loading the suppression list before anything else. Sequences themselves don't transfer automatically between tools, so plan to rebuild them, and let active lemlist sequences finish before switching to avoid double-contacting prospects. Reconnecting the same mailboxes preserves your sender reputation.

### Is lemlist still worth it in 2026?

For solo users who lean heavily on image and video personalisation, lemlist is still one of the best tools available — it pioneered that category and does it well. The value case weakens as your team grows, because every additional person multiplies the monthly cost, and reviewers report real spend often exceeding the sticker price once credits and add-ons are included.

### Do these alternatives offer free trials?

Most do. Velox House offers a 21-day free trial on every plan with no contract; Instantly, Smartlead and Saleshandy offer trials or free tiers; Apollo has a permanent free plan. Trial two tools against the same small lead list — reply rate and deliverability, not feature lists, should decide it.
