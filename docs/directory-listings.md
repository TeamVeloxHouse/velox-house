### Long description (directory "about" fields)
Velox House is an AI-powered outbound platform for founders, B2B sales teams and agencies. Instead of stitching together a database, a sequencer and a warm-up tool, you describe your ideal customer in plain English and Velox AI plans the whole campaign: it expands your ICP into dozens of targeted searches, builds a ranked market of matching companies, SMTP-verifies every contact before import, researches each business, writes personalised cold emails and LinkedIn messages, and — once you approve — sends them every day on a natural, inbox-safe schedule from your own mailbox (Google, Microsoft 365 or SMTP).

Set a goal like "50 replies a week" and the autopilot keeps finding leads and sending until it's hit. An AI copilot explains your numbers, runs campaign autopsies and drafts replies in your shared inbox. Deliverability tooling (SPF/DKIM/DMARC checks and a pre-send spam checker) keeps your domain safe, and email sending is unlimited on every plan.

Velox House is UK-built with GBP pricing: plans from £19.99/month, a LinkedIn seat included from the £49.99 Growth plan, and a 21-day free trial on everything. No contracts.

## Review generation (the part that compounds)
Directories only pay off with reviews. After launch: add a post-win in-app prompt or a
short email to happy users asking for a G2/Trustpilot review (G2 allows incentivised
review campaigns via their vendor programme; Trustpilot invitations must go to real
customers). 10-15 genuine reviews is the threshold where category pages start showing
the product.
