01 · Executive summary

A business that operates itself, on your command.

TellOvi is an AI-operated business operating system. A single platform, built on one shared data spine, where an AI operator named Ovi runs the entire commercial process — from researching and generating leads, through sales, quoting and ordering, all the way to fulfilment and delivery — while human teams collaborate on the same records and direct Ovi in plain language.

Today a growing company stitches together ten or more disconnected tools — a CRM, a prospecting tool, an email platform, a BI dashboard, a quoting app, an ordering system, a scheduling tool, a project tracker and a team chat — and spends enormous human effort being the glue between them. Their "AI" is a chatbot bolted onto the side of one box, unable to see or touch the rest. The work of joining it all up still falls on people.

TellOvi collapses that entire stack into one platform and hands the joining-up to Ovi. Because every stage lives on one data model, Ovi acts with full context the whole way through: it researches a market and surfaces genuinely qualified leads, runs the outreach, works the pipeline, builds the quote, places the order, sources the product, schedules the delivery by location, and reports on all of it — and it does this inside the team chat, taking action straight off the conversation.

Not software you operate. A business that operates itself, on your command.

The wedge is simple and hard to copy: end-to-end on one spine. Point tools automate a slice; TellOvi owns the whole thread, so its AI is an operator, not an assistant. Every action across the lifecycle feeds a compounding data flywheel that makes the next research, score, price and route smarter — an advantage fragmented incumbents structurally cannot build.

Our edge is focus and architecture — we're the only end-to-end AI operator built for the businesses the giants ignore: SMB and mid-market firms that both sell and deliver — installers, trades, agencies and wholesalers. 02 · The idea

A new category: the Agentic Business Operating System.

Every previous wave of business software digitised a function — a place to store contacts, a place to send email, a place to track jobs. The next wave doesn't digitise a function. It operates the whole business.

TellOvi's thesis is that the unit of software is no longer the app you open — it's the operator you instruct. You don't navigate menus, tabs and settings across ten products; you tell Ovi what you want and it does the work across all of them, because there is no "them" — there is one system. The brand name is the interface: tell Ovi.

Three shifts make this the right idea at the right moment:

SHIFT 01

AI can now operate

Frontier models can reliably plan, chain tools and take multi-step action. That is the line between a chatbot that answers and an operator that does the job.

SHIFT 02

Buyers want one system

Budgets are consolidating. Teams are tired of paying for ten seats and doing the integration by hand. One platform that does the work beats a stack you assemble.

SHIFT 03

Ops for everyone

Small teams can't hire analysts, SDRs and coordinators. Ovi gives a five-person company an entire operations department inside a text box.

The unit of software is no longer the app you open. It's the operator you instruct.

03 · The problem

The modern business is a pile of tools held together by people.

Ask any growing company how a customer actually flows through their business and you'll hear a list of products, not a process. Each tool owns one slice, none of them talk properly, and the humans are the integration layer.

Fragmented data

The lead, the deal, the quote, the order and the delivery live in different systems. No one place knows the whole story, so no AI can either. Reporting means exporting and stitching spreadsheets.

Manual glue

People re-key the same customer into five tools, chase updates across apps, and copy answers between chat, CRM and dashboards. Most of the working day is coordination, not value.

Bolt-on AI

The "AI" is a sidebar in one product. It can draft an email but can't see the pipeline, can't place an order, can't schedule the install. It assists; it doesn't operate.

Ten bills, one job

CRM + prospecting + email + BI + quoting + ordering + scheduling + chat is a stack of subscriptions and logins — expensive to buy, painful to run, impossible to make coherent.

The result: growth is capped by headcount, because scaling the business means scaling the human glue. The problem isn't a missing feature in any one tool — it's that no tool owns the whole process, so intelligence has nowhere to stand.

05 · How Ovi operates

The operating model: one spine, one operator, many hands.

TellOvi is architected so that intelligence has somewhere to stand and something to do. Four layers make Ovi a true operator rather than a chat window.

LAYER 01

The data spine

A single, shared model of the whole business — accounts, people, deals, quotes, orders, products, jobs, schedules, conversations. Every module reads and writes the same objects, so there is one source of truth and cross-links happen for free (a won deal becomes an order becomes a scheduled delivery).

LAYER 02

The agent runtime

Ovi is a network of specialised agents over that spine — a research agent, an outreach agent, a pricing agent, a scheduling agent — orchestrated by a planner. It breaks a request into steps, calls real tools, and shows its working as it goes.

LAYER 03

Actions, not answers

Ovi's tools do things: create a task, send a sequence, generate a quote, raise a purchase order, book an install, pull a report. Every action is a real, permissioned mutation of the spine — auditable and reversible.

LAYER 04

Governance & trust

Role-based permissions, approval gates for sensitive actions (sending, spending, deleting), a full audit trail, and "show your work" streaming so teams can see and trust what Ovi did. AI is powerful and accountable.

Ovi is addressable from anywhere the same way — the CRM record, the website chatbot, the quote screen, the delivery board, and the team channel are all just surfaces onto the same operator. Ask it a question and it answers from live data; give it a job and it carries it out, carrying full context between surfaces.

07 · Deep dive — the research lead engine

Leads with a reason to buy, not rows in a list.

Lead generation is where TellOvi's advantage begins, and it's built differently on purpose. The market is full of databases that sell you contacts. We don't sell contacts — Ovi researches its way to the right opportunity and hands you a lead that already carries its own justification.

The engine runs a research loop, not a lookup:

Ovi · define

Learn what a good customer actually looks like

Ovi builds your ICP from your real won deals and your best accounts — the firmographics, the shapes, the tells — not a generic filter you guess at.

Ovi · scan

Watch the market for buying signals

It monitors for triggers that mean "now" — expansion, hiring, funding, new premises, tech or supplier changes, tender activity — the events that precede a purchase.

Ovi · research

Investigate each candidate in depth

For a promising account, Ovi reads around the company, identifies the right people, understands their situation, and drafts the specific reason this is a fit right now.

Ovi · qualify

Score fit + timing, and write the "why now"

Each lead lands with a fit score, a timing score and a one-line rationale a rep can act on immediately — and it's de-duplicated against the live spine so no one works it twice.

Ovi · learn

Feed outcomes back into the model

Which leads converted, which stalled, which delivered profitably — every outcome sharpens the next round of targeting. The engine gets better the more you sell.

A database gives you a list. Ovi gives you a shortlist that already knows why it's on the list.

Worked example — a domestic solar & battery installer

To make it concrete, here is a real-world vertical: an MCS-certified installer selling and fitting solar PV and battery storage to homeowners. This is exactly the kind of "sell and deliver" SMB the incumbents don't serve — and where owning the whole thread is transformative. One job, from a rooftop Ovi has never seen to a commissioned, exporting system.

OviResearch + OviLeads

Finds the right roofs, with a reason

Ovi builds the ICP from the installer's best past jobs and watches for intent — homeowners in high-irradiance postcodes with suitable south/east-west roofs, recent EPC updates, EV ownership and rising-bill signals — queuing each with a fit score and a written "why now."

OviChat + OviReach

Qualifies and books the survey

A homeowner lands on the website; OviChat asks the right questions — roof type, orientation, typical bill, ownership, battery interest — and books a survey. Warm leads that don't convert get a personalised nurture sequence from OviReach.

OviQuote

Designs the system and prices it

From the survey, Ovi sizes the array (e.g. a 4.2 kWp system with a 5 kWh battery), runs the pricing calculator — panels, inverter, battery, scaffolding, MCS and labour — and generates a branded proposal showing annual savings, SEG export income and payback. Discounts route to Teams for approval, then it goes for e-signature.

OviOrder + OviResearch

Sources the kit and raises POs

The won quote becomes an order. Ovi researches and selects panels, inverter and battery against price, stock and lead time, raises the supplier purchase orders, and flags anything that would slip the install date.

OviDiary

Schedules by postcode to cut van miles

Ovi clusters the survey and install against jobs in the same area, matches an MCS-certified team with capacity, and books scaffolding to arrive ahead of the crew — minimising travel and idle days across the week's route.

OviDeliver

Installs, certifies and switches on

The install becomes a live project: scaffold, fit, and commission, with the DNO / G99 application, MCS certificate and handover pack tracked as milestones — through to the SEG export tariff being set up so the customer starts earning.

OviAnalytics + OviTeams

Learns and compounds

Margin per install, install-days per job and route efficiency feed analytics; the profitable outcome sharpens the next round of roof targeting. When the owner asks in chat "how many installs are booked this month?", Ovi answers from live data — instantly.

10 · The human + AI model

Collaborative by design — people and Ovi on the same objects.

TellOvi is not an autopilot that locks people out, and not a copilot that only suggests. It's a shared workspace where humans and Ovi operate the same records — each doing what they're best at, each able to see and adjust the other's work.

Direct in plain language

Anyone can tell Ovi what they want — in a record, a screen or a channel — without learning a new interface. The instruction is the UI.

Work the same records

A rep and Ovi edit the same deal; ops and Ovi share the same delivery board. No hand-off files, no duplicate state — one object, many hands.

Trust through transparency

Ovi streams its steps, asks before sensitive actions, and logs everything. People stay in control while Ovi carries the load.

Cross-team by default

Sales, ops, delivery and leadership see one connected process, not siloed tools. A change in one place is visible everywhere it matters — the coordination tax disappears.

Roles & permissions

Who can see what, who can approve what, and what Ovi may do on its own versus with sign-off — configured per team, so autonomy scales safely with trust.
