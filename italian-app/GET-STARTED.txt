IMPARA! — Learn Italian  🇮🇹
============================

This folder IS the app. No installing, no build step.

--------------------------------------------------------------
TRY IT RIGHT NOW (on this computer)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click  index.html  — it opens in your web browser and works.
(Audio and all lessons work. "Add to phone" + full offline mode
 need it to be hosted online — see below.)

--------------------------------------------------------------
PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE (free, ~2 minutes, no app store)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. On your computer, go to:   https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag THIS "italian-app" folder onto that web page.
   (You may need a free Netlify account — sign up takes a moment.)
3. Netlify gives you a live web link (e.g. something.netlify.app).
4. Open that link ON YOUR PHONE, then:
      iPhone  : Share button  ->  "Add to Home Screen"
      Android : menu (⋮)       ->  "Install app"
5. It now opens full-screen like a normal app and works offline.

--------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT'S INSIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Guided path: beginner (A1) to advanced (C1)
- Exercises: multiple choice, word-bank, typing, listening, match
- XP, streaks, hearts, crowns, spaced-repetition practice
- "Create" tab: type ANY word/phrase/sentence -> instant lesson
  (needs internet the first time for a new word; then saved offline)

Your progress is saved privately on your own device only.

--------------------------------------------------------------
ADD YOUR OWN CONTENT
--------------------------------------------------------------
Open  js/course-extra.js  in any text editor and copy the pattern
to add lessons. Exercises build themselves from the words you add.
